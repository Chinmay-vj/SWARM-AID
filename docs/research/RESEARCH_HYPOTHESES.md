# Prospective hypotheses

| ID | Paired contrast | Predicted direction | Evidence against |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Hybrid versus independent search at equal fleet size | Higher unique coverage by horizon | Nonpositive paired coverage difference |
| H2 | Hybrid versus centralized under command outage | Smaller reduction in completion relative to each method's healthy condition | Equal or larger reduction |
| H3 | Visible plus thermal versus visible alone at fixed threshold | Higher recall under degraded visibility | Recall unchanged or lower |
| H4 | Independent multidrone evidence versus first-drone evidence on the same cases | Lower unsupported recommendation rate | Rate unchanged or higher |

Record secondary costs alongside primary outcomes. Higher recall can coincide with more false alarms; greater coverage can consume more energy. Freeze thresholds before the held-out run. Treat MAPPO and QMIX comparisons as exploratory until policies and training budgets exist. Evaluate paired uncertainty intervals and effect sizes before interpreting any predicted direction.
