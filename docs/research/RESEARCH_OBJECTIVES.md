# Research objectives

Study the trade-off between coverage, timeliness, evidence quality and communication cost in simulated civilian search. The unit of comparison is a complete seeded mission. Preserve failures, incomplete runs and undetected synthetic persons.

| Question | Controlled intervention | Primary outcome |
|---|---|---|
| How does task allocation change search? | Independent, conventional partition, centralized, decentralized and hybrid allocation | Coverage by horizon and time to discovery |
| How sensitive is coordination to loss? | Hold scenario and allocation fixed; vary delivery and outages | Objective completion under disruption |
| Does additional sensing help? | Single visible or thermal modality versus both | Precision, recall and false alarms |
| Does additional evidence improve assessment? | First reporting drone alone versus all independent captures | Incident assessment accuracy and unsupported proposals |
| Can learned coordination improve on rules? | Future MAPPO or QMIX adapter under matched evaluation constraints | Same endpoints and resource budgets |

Scope conclusions to the configured simulator, sensor model and human-review protocol. No measured outcome, trained policy or novelty claim is available in Phase 1.
