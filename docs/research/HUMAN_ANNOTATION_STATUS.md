# Independent annotation status

The audit checked external `annotations/model2` for first-look and adjudicated forms. It found **zero independent submissions, zero adjudicated revisions, zero approved records, zero recorded rejections and zero independently labeled ambiguous cases**. The frozen target vocabulary contains pending, reviewed and ambiguous status. Absence of a rejection is distinct from acceptance.

All 510 pilot and 4,975 field targets remain pending. The 100 first-look forms correspond to unique field samples from 69 episodes. Each matched a blank revision-zero unresolved Target (taxonomy 1.0) with its projected feature sample ID. Initial forms omit current rule assessment, relationship, urgency, incident and recommendation. The receipt still contains the original Model 1 finding. Sixty-nine episodes measure sampling coverage, not reviewer independence. Six ambiguity-stratum selections are weak candidates.

1. Assign two distinct reviewers to the same held-out cohort. Open only the first-look queue and prior operational context. Hide the current baseline and simulator truth.
2. Independently record native evidence IDs, uncertainty, missing inputs, assessment and urgency or abstention. Submit attributable revision-one decisions. First-look judgments remain separate and immutable.
3. Compare common independently rated IDs. Report paired sample size, raw agreement, class disagreements and Cohen’s kappa where defined. Review evidence attribution and recommendation disagreement separately. All measures are currently unavailable.
4. After both submissions, adjudicate disagreements against the original as-of evidence, incident revision, allowed action and expiry. Record a new revision with rationale. Keep protected evaluation decisions separate from development.

A future weak-supervision study could use eligible development rules for imitation with provenance and controlled-parent group weights. Freeze the weak labeling policy before measuring performance on independently labeled, group-disjoint evaluation episodes. This permits a prespecified stratified human sample instead of requiring annotation of every candidate.
