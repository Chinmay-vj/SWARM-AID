# Version 2 field scenarios

The six `*_field.json` files extend the existing Scenario contract with `field_profile`. Version 2 requires the profile. Version 1 excludes it. Dimensions, sectors, terrain, event steps, civilian count, horizon, seed, dropout, latency and router conditions continue through the existing loader and backend whitelist. A profile adds a difficulty, optional initial search cells, optional odd-numbered-agent modality exclusions and disjoint, half-open observation windows. A window names its condition, start and stop ticks, active radius cap and disabled modalities. Time is integer mission ticks, converted to milliseconds by `tick_ms`. Radius is Manhattan distance in cells. [Scenario configuration](SCENARIO_CONFIGURATION.md)

| Fixture | Timed ground changes | Observation or communication behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Flood | Two cells inundate at ticks 4 and 9, a route blocks at 15 and silt appears at 23. | Rising-water window begins at 4, disables image and caps radius at 2. Water and silt are categorical states. |
| Wildfire | Smoke at 5, fire at 10, burned terrain at 17 and a second smoke cell at 24. | Smoke-front window starts at 6, disables image and caps radius at 1. Thermal readings on fire can emit a qualitative anomaly. |
| Earthquake | A structure becomes damaged at 6, debris appears at 12 and access blocks at 20. | Aftershock window starts at 7, disables thermal and caps radius at 1. Frames arrive two ticks after capture. |
| Cyclone | A radio-weak cell forms at 4, infrastructure damage occurs at 11 and the road returns at 20. | A squall starting at 3 reduces the footprint to the observer's cell and disables image. Command goes offline at ticks 7–10. Base delay is three ticks with configured jitter. |
| Landslide | Four successive downslope cells become debris or blocked at 8, 12, 16 and 22. | A slope-failure window starting at 9 disables environmental readings and caps radius at 1. The router applies duplicate and jitter settings. |
| Missing person | Brush obstructs the trail at 8 and the trail reopens at 18. | One civilian starts at a seeded location within seven candidate cells and can move. Dusk starts at 5, disables thermal and caps radius at 1. False-positive probability is 0.04 per eligible cell/modality. |

All windows last nine ticks. The default field grid is 11 by 9 with 38 ticks of 100 ms. The simulator applies scheduled tile changes after movement and before the new capture. Blocked/debris/damaged cells constrain entry. Changing terrain does not imply a measured water level, temperature, wind speed, injury or entrapment. Missing-person search uses restricted local views. The configured initial region is a simulator placement prior; published mission objectives contain no civilian coordinates. The standard human-review path handles possible sightings.

**Difficulty and sensing.** Easy/moderate/difficult add 0/0.04/0.08 to modality dropout and 0/0.12/0.24 to missed-detection probability, capped at 1. The synthetic possible-person score starts at 0.60/0.52/0.44 and falls by 0.08 during an observation window. Noise can obscure a terrain label or displace a candidate to the observer cell. Odd-numbered field agents lack thermal. GPS, IMU and text use the existing telemetry/mission contracts. Audio has no Evidence wire type in this version and yields zero audio candidates. No raw media, synchronization or calibrated probability is implied.

**Transport and identity.** The integrated router uses existing seeded loss, jitter, duplicates, temporary endpoint loss and reconnection. Jitter can reorder delivery. Packet duplication and application acceptance are counted separately. Field evidence IDs include scenario, seed, fleet, difficulty, observing agent, tick, modality and reading position. Relaying retains evidence identity. Civilian placement, hazards, civilian motion, modality draws and network events use separate seeded streams. Tile events follow their configured schedule. Release metadata records event tick, milliseconds, cell and fixture provenance. Transport timings describe a symbolic network. [Communication architecture](../PHASE_9_COMPLETION_REPORT.md)
